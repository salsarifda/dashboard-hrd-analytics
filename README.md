# dashboard-hrd-analytics
Power BI HR Analytics dashboard analyzing employee attrition patterns across demographics, job roles, compensation, overtime, satisfaction, and career progression.

The dashboard provides an overview of workforce composition and allows users to interactively explore attrition patterns across departments, job roles, salary categories, overtime status, job satisfaction, and years since the last promotion.

Key questions addressed:
1.Which departments and job roles have higher employee attrition rates?
2. Which age groups have higher employee attrition rates?
3. Is career progression, measured by time since the last promotion, associated with employee attrition?
4. How does salary category relate to employee attrition?
5. Does job satisfaction differ between employees who stay and those who leave?
6. Is distance from home associated with employee attrition rates?
7. Is overtime associated with higher employee attrition rates?
8. Is work-life balance associated with employee attrition rates?
